# Validation Strategy

## Three-Layer Validation Process

1.  **Self-Test**: Authors review their own work against a checklist.
2.  **Peer Review**: Content reviewed by fellow authors/experts.
3.  **Community Review**: Broader community feedback solicited.

## Reader Testing Protocol
-   Measure time to complete exercises.
-   Collect confusion points from readers.
-   Aim for 90% completion for basic exercises.
-   Aim for 70% completion for advanced challenges.
-   Target <3 confusion points per chapter.
